Sheet music isn't sexy, but that wouldn't stop this company from expanding sheet music availability with a unified set of apps across Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android devices.

In an industry where modernization was lagging behind, we worked to expand the brand with intuitive, user-friendly digital experiences for musicians.

The result is a comprehensive redesign that gives customers an unmistakable experience of playing with Musicnotes.
